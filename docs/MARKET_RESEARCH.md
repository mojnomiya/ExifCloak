## Proposed Name: **Cloak**

**Rejected (taken):** Cloaki (YouTube privacy), Cloaked (identity protection), Cloaky (screen privacy)

## Final Name Recommendation: **ExifCloak**

### Why "ExifCloak"
- **Meaning:** "Cloak" = to hide, conceal, make invisible. You cloak your image's identity.
- **Unique:** Not taken by any competitor in this space
- **Memorable:** Two syllables, easy to say, easy to spell
- **SEO-friendly:** Contains "Exif" which is the primary search term
- **Cross-platform ready:** Works as a brand for macOS + Windows + web
- **Domain potential:** exifcloak.com / exifcloak.app likely available
- **Positioning:** Not just a "cleaner" or "stripper" — it's a cloaking tool (implies sophistication: hide, replace, disguise metadata rather than just delete)

### Tagline Options
1. "Cloak your images. Own your privacy."
2. "Your images, your metadata, your rules."
3. "See it. Edit it. Cloak it."
